BEFORE YOU PAY

Invoice & payment review

Describe a change or paste non-sensitive invoice wording. Never enter full account numbers, passwords, or private customer data.

Always confirm changed payment details using a phone number you already trust—not a number in the unexpected message.

Check a message or payment request

Paste suspicious text below. Don’t include passwords or private customer or bank information.

Ransomware: reduce the risk

Keep devices and software updated, use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication where available, and keep backups separate from everyday devices. Be cautious with unexpected attachments and links.

If you suspect an attack: stop using the affected device for business tasks, contact your organisation’s IT/security support immediately, and follow your incident plan. Don’t try to investigate or pay a demand on your own.
